# New Employee Onboarding Guide — Acme Tech

## Your first day

Welcome to Acme Tech. On your first day, arrive at the reception desk at 9:00 to collect your access badge and meet the people team. Bring a government-issued photo ID and, if you are hired under a CLT contract, the documents listed in your offer letter. The badge gives access to the office floors and to the cafeteria, and it must be worn visibly while you are in the building.

After the welcome session, the IT team will hand over your laptop and walk you through the initial setup: email, the company chat, the VPN client and the password manager. Plan about two hours for this step. By the end of the day you should be able to sign in to all of the core tools, so tell your buddy right away if something does not work.

## Your buddy

Each new employee is paired with a buddy, an experienced colleague who is not your manager. The buddy program lasts 90 days. Your buddy answers day-to-day questions, introduces you to other teams and helps you understand how things really work, which is often different from what is written in the documents.

You and your buddy should meet once a week during the first month, and every two weeks after that. These meetings are informal, but the people team asks both of you to fill in a short feedback form at the end of the 90 days.

## Equipment and workspace

Every employee receives a laptop, and monitors and keyboards can be requested for the office desk or for the home office. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. If your equipment breaks, open a helpdesk ticket and a replacement is lent to you while the repair is done. Personal use of company equipment is allowed in moderation, as long as the security rules are respected.

The office is open from 7:00 to 20:00 on business days. Meeting rooms are booked in the calendar tool, and a booking that is not used within 10 minutes is released automatically for other people.

## Leave and time off

| Type of leave | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Annual vacation | 30 calendar days per 12 months |
| Sick leave without a medical certificate | up to 3 days per year |
| Study day for exams | 1 day per exam |
| Volunteer day | 1 day per year |

Sick leave of more than 3 days requires a medical certificate, which must be uploaded to the people portal within 48 hours. Vacation requests are made in the portal and need manager approval; see the Employee Handbook for the rules on splitting vacation periods.

## Mandatory training

During the first 30 days you must complete three online courses: security awareness, the code of conduct and the workplace safety course. The completion of each course is tracked automatically in the learning portal, and your manager receives a reminder if any of them is still pending after 3 weeks. Your probation period review takes place at the end of the 90 days, and it considers your progress, the feedback from your buddy and the completion of the mandatory training.
